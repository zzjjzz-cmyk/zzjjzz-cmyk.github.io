Amnesia (勿忘我)

Amnesia is a story-driven puzzle game about a brother and sister, told through two different perspectives. Players go through the same events from Memor’s and Fotreg’s points of view, and only later realize that what they believed at first was not the full truth. This project was really about building up emotion, guiding assumptions, and then using the final reveal to make everything click.

What I focused on:

  • Designing the two-perspective narrative structure and final reveal
  • Controlling pacing so players gradually question what they think is happening
  • Making the gameplay and level goals support the emotional beats of the story
  • Helping lead team collaboration across design, art, and implementation

P3 Gold - Bargain Brawl

Bargain Brawl is a chaotic two-player party game set in a supermarket. The goal sounds simple: finish your shopping first. The problem is that the other player is also free to ram into you, steal space, throw things around, and make your life miserable. I wanted this game to feel silly, competitive, and just a little bit mean in the best way.

What I focused on:

  • Designing the core loop of shopping, racing, and messing with the other player
  • Building item interactions like pickup, checkout, and offensive or defensive item use
  • Creating split-screen presentation, tutorial flow, and end-of-match results
  • Iterating on controls, map readability, and game rules through playtesting
  • Play Bargain Brawl

P2 Gold - Inside Mirror

Inside Mirror is a 3D puzzle game built around the relationship between a real world and a mirrored world. The player is the only light source in reality, while objects in the mirror world react in the opposite way. A lot of the fun here came from teaching the player a strange set of rules, then letting them slowly realize how much they could do with those rules once they understood them.

What I focused on:

  • Designing puzzles around symmetry, inversion, and player understanding
  • Building the mirror world activation and deactivation system
  • Using event-driven interactions to keep puzzle logic modular and readable
  • Creating camera transitions that make switching between worlds feel intuitive
  • Play Inside Mirror

P1 Gold – The Legend of Zelda Remake

This project started as a remake of classic Zelda-style dungeon gameplay, but for me it was really a chance to study why that formula feels so good in the first place. I rebuilt the core movement, combat, and enemy interactions in Unity, with a big focus on making everything feel clear, responsive, and satisfying.

What I focused on:

  • Recreating player movement and directional combat with solid game feel
  • Building enemy AI behaviors like patrol, chase, and attack
  • Implementing hit detection and damage feedback that feels readable
  • Improving the prototype through quick playtesting and iteration
